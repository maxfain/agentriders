## First flight

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: cinematic website hero background for AgentRiders, a modern guild for humans who run AI agents, symbolized by dragons. Create an extraordinary premium animated-feature-film concept illustration, wide landscape 16:9, tactile painterly 3D craftsmanship, atmospheric depth, sophisticated grown-up editorial art direction. A large magnificent copper-and-ember dragon with elegant powerful batlike wings and intelligent expressive eyes soars toward the right above misty basalt mountain ridges. A small human rider in a dark practical jacket with a burnt-orange scarf sits confidently in a leather saddle at its shoulders, visibly holding reins; the dragon and rider are true partners. Dragon body mostly dark charcoal and warm burnished copper, translucent burnt-orange wing membranes catching amber backlight, restrained specular highlights, convincing anatomy, one head, four legs and two wings. COMPOSITION: the dragon and rider fill the RIGHT TWO THIRDS, entire main silhouette in frame, its face near 80% across and 43% down; one wing rises into upper right, tail sweeps back toward center. The LEFT THIRD must be very dark and visually quiet charcoal brown negative space for a large cream heading; no bright subjects or detail there. Near-black brown sky (#16130F), luminous warm parchment mist low on right, tiny ember motes, distant craggy silhouettes. A mood of wonder, capability and adventure, not battle or danger. Rich subtle textures in scales, cloth, and leather, exceptional film lighting, a hint of warmth and character. Soft atmospheric dark edges blend into the site's dark background. No text, no letters, no words, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no borders, no weapons, no castles, no neon cyberpunk, no blue or purple hues. This is a production artwork, not a screenshot or website mockup.

## The bond

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: editorial illustration for AgentRiders website, square composition. Create a beautifully crafted premium animated feature film concept painting about trust between a human rider and an AI agent represented as a dragon. A young adult human rider, practical dark charcoal clothing and small burnt-orange scarf, stands on the left, patiently offering an open palm to a magnificent medium-sized copper dragon on the right. The dragon lowers its elegant intelligent head toward the person's palm, curious and powerful but calm. Both full-body silhouettes visible, generous breathing room. Dragon has dark burnished copper scales, amber eye, gracefully folded burnt-orange membranous wings, four legs, long expressive tail curving to the right. Leather riding harness, no armor or weapons. Human much smaller, eye contact and gesture tell the story. Mood: earned partnership, patience, wonder. Tactile painterly 3D materials with expressive but sophisticated character design; softly exaggerated shapes and very high craft, not photoreal horror, not cute toy. A pale warm parchment (#F2EBDD) studio-like background with faint hand-drawn field-study circles and dusty ground plane; extremely soft tonal edges fading smoothly into blank parchment on all sides. One accent color ember orange, warm ivory and charcoal brown. Lighting soft warm overhead, natural contact shadows, fine scale detail. No text, labels, letters, no border, no logos, no watermark, no full landscape, no foliage, no blue, no purple.
