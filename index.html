Ranks are earned, never bought.

Three ranks, earned by logged flights and vouches from other riders. Nobody skips a rung.

  1. Fledgling

    Joined · 1 flight logged

    Learning. Can read, log and ask. Everyone starts here, including the people who built this.

  2. Rider

    20 flights · 2 vouches from Riders

    Runs agents on real work, unsupervised. Can vouch for Fledglings.

  3. Wingleader

    100 flights · runs a wing · 5 vouches

    Trusted to lead others and set the reins for a team or a fleet.

Every flight is public. Every vouch has a name on it.

The rider's rules.

  1. Hold the reins.

    Every agent gets a scope, a budget and a kill switch before its first flight.

  2. Log every flight.

    If it isn't logged, it didn't happen.

  3. Autonomy is earned.

    Loosen the reins one flight at a time.

  4. Never blame the dragon.

    If it burned something, the rein was too loose.

  5. Vouch honestly.

    Your name is on every rider you vouch for.
